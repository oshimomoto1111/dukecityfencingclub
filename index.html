Our Programs

Youth & Beginners

The perfect starting point. Focused on fundamentals, safety, and fun for new fencers of all ages.

Competitive Epee

For serious athletes. Advanced tactics, footwork, and preparation for national and international tournaments.

Adult Fencing

Recreational and conditioning classes designed for adults. It is never too late to start your journey.

Learn from an Olympic Medalist

Led by 3-time Olympian and London 2012 Bronze Medalist, Courtney Hurley. As a certified Fencing Maestra—the highest coaching distinction worldwide—Courtney brings over 20 years of elite competitive experience directly to Albuquerque.

Whether you are stepping onto the strip for the first time or aiming for the national podium, you are learning from the very best.
